Privacy Policy

App: Dizaro  |  Last updated: May 13, 2026  |  Effective date: May 13, 2026

Dizaro ("we", "our", or "us") is operated by Dmitriy Anishchenkov, an individual developer based in Daejeon, South Korea. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information when you use the Dizaro mobile application ("App").

By using Dizaro, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only what is necessary to provide the App's functionality:

  • Account information: When you register, we collect your email address and, if you use Sign in with Apple or Google, your name and email as provided by those services.
  • Financial data you enter: Transactions, amounts, categories, budgets, financial goals, subscriptions, and notes that you manually input into the App. This data belongs to you.
  • Receipt photos: If you attach a photo to a transaction, that image is stored securely in your account.
  • Device information: Basic device identifiers and iOS version, used only for crash reporting and compatibility.
  • Usage data: Anonymous analytics on which features are used (no personal financial data is included).

We do not collect your bank credentials, payment card numbers, or connect directly to your bank accounts.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide, maintain, and improve the App's features
  • To sync your data across your devices
  • To send important notifications (budget alerts, subscription reminders) if you have enabled them
  • To process your in-app subscription via Apple In-App Purchase
  • To respond to your support requests
  • To comply with legal obligations

We do not sell your personal data to third parties. We do not use your financial data for advertising.

3. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using Supabase, a cloud database provider. Data is encrypted in transit (TLS/SSL) and at rest. Supabase infrastructure is hosted on AWS in secure data centers.

We implement industry-standard security measures. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

4. Third-Party Services

Dizaro uses the following third-party services, each governed by their own privacy policies:

  • Supabase – database and authentication (supabase.com/privacy)
  • Apple Sign In – optional authentication (apple.com/legal/privacy)
  • Google Sign In – optional authentication (policies.google.com/privacy)
  • Apple In-App Purchase – subscription payments (apple.com/legal/privacy)

We do not share your personal financial data with any of these services.

5. Subscriptions and Payments

Dizaro offers optional paid subscriptions (Monthly and Annual) processed entirely through Apple In-App Purchase. We do not collect or store your payment card information. All billing is managed by Apple. For refund requests or subscription management, please use your Apple ID settings or contact Apple Support.

6. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, your personal data and financial records are permanently deleted from our servers within 30 days. Receipt photos are deleted immediately upon account deletion.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights:

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Correction: Update inaccurate data (editable directly in the App)
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your account and all associated data
  • Portability: Export your transaction data (CSV/PDF export available in the App)
  • Opt-out of notifications: Disable push notifications at any time in iOS Settings

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at dimasky228@gmail.com.

8. Children's Privacy

Dizaro is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

9. International Users

Dizaro is operated from South Korea. If you access the App from outside South Korea, your data may be transferred to and processed in South Korea or in the country where our cloud provider's servers are located (United States). By using the App, you consent to this transfer.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, via in-app notification. Your continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
